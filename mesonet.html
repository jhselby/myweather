Feed health

  • Tempest is the only live station feed. It keeps its last good payload on a failed fetch, marks a run "degraded" under 75% valid stations, and the daily digest carries a Tempest line.
  • On a failed tick the forecast's start gets no last-good station input.
  • Tempest is a single point of failure: it depends on the WeatherFlow sign-in (anonymous access closed 10-15, v0.7.67). No backup source: CWOP was checked on 11-04 and has about one station near the Cove.
  • Per-station uptime is the last column of the Tempest table.

How it's built

From station to Mesonet

  1. Correct each station. Every station is compared with the other stations over the past weeks, separately by day and by night. A station that runs warm is corrected down by that amount. No forecast is involved.
  2. Drop outliers. Within each compass direction from the Cove, a reading far from its neighbours is set aside.
  3. Weight by distance. Nearer stations count more, and stations at a very different height count less.
  4. Balance the directions. Each compass direction is averaged first, then the directions are averaged, so a cluster of stations on one side cannot dominate.

How each field is combined (L2)

The forecast starts from the Mesonet's current reading. Each field is combined in its own way, suited to how noisy that field is. How much of the reading the forecast keeps at each lead time is on the Model page.

Temperature, humidity, pressure (additive bias): each station's offset (rolling 48 h) → per direction, a 1/distance² × exp(−|elevation difference|/30) weighted (station − model) gap → the plain mean across directions that have stations. Temperature and humidity are then scaled by a confidence K (Model page); pressure applies in full.
Wind and gusts (direct selection): no per-station calibration and no additive bias. Per direction, the maximum gust; then the median across directions; blended linearly into the next 24 h (100% now → 0% at 24 h). Airport floor: if KBOS and KBVY both exceed 1.4× the direction median, their median is used. Direction is rejected if it is more than 60° off the airport, buoy and Tempest consensus. Gust is never below wind.
Cloud cover (Kalman METAR blend): KBOS and KBVY sky reports (BKN/SCT/FEW/OVC → percent, low/mid/high) blended against HRRR's current hour. K = 0.90 if the airports agree within 20 points, 0.70 at 20–40, 0.50 above 40, 0.35 with one source.
