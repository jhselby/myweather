The Mesonet value uses station readings only. No forecast model goes into it. The model column shows what the forecast model (HRRR) has for this hour, for comparison.

The stations

Each dot is a Tempest station; tap one for its name and reading. Colour is the station's correction right now: red reads warm and is corrected down, blue reads cold and is corrected up. The white dot is the Cove.

How we got this

  1. Correct each station. Every station is compared with the other stations over the past weeks, separately by day and by night. A station that runs warm is corrected down by that amount. No forecast is involved.
  2. Drop outliers. Within each compass direction from the Cove, a reading far from its neighbours is set aside.
  3. Weight by distance. Nearer stations count more, and stations at a very different height count less.
  4. Balance the directions. Each compass direction is averaged first, then the directions are averaged, so a cluster of stations on one side cannot dominate.
